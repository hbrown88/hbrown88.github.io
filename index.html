03 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

What I want
to work on.

I’m a computer science student at Georgia Tech, concentrating in Artificial Intelligence and Systems & Architecture. I’m especially interested in embedded AI systems and AI development: building intelligent software and bringing it into devices that interact with the world. I want my work to make life easier for people with fewer resources—through education, access to new technology, or attention to social problems.

I’m also concerned by how easily people trust AI-generated content. One idea I’m exploring is a browser extension that flags potentially AI-generated videos and helps viewers question what they’re seeing. It’s an idea, not a finished project. I care about whether software improves someone’s life, beyond whether it can turn a profit.
